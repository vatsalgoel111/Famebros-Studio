# Famebros Studio — Project Brief

## Overview
Famebros Studio is a Mumbai social media and creative studio that runs the Instagram presence of 50+ brands across jewellery, fashion, hospitality, retail, and more.

## Core Concept: "Already In Your Feed"
Famebros' work lives on other brands' Instagram accounts, so the site is a wall of the real feeds they run. 
Recurring motif: a small black-on-yellow **"Managed by Famebros"** credit pill on every piece of work.

## Hero Headline
> "You've already seen our work."

## Sections
1. **Hero** (`#hero`): Bold introduction establishing the studio's presence in Mumbai and leading into the feed wall.
2. **The Wall** (`#wall`): Client feed mosaic with industry filter pills and bottom sheets showing client context, services, and live handles.
3. **The Breakdown** (`#breakdown`): Detailed case studies presented as reels with a scrub bar and timestamped markers (Hook, Craft, Beat, CTA).
4. **One Month** (`#one-month`): Transparent display of one real client's month-long content calendar.
5. **"Managed by" Means** (`#managed-by`): Five distinct service rows — Shoot, Cut, Post, Answer comments, Send numbers.
6. **The Bros** (`#crew`): The studio crew and directors behind the camera and edit desks.
7. **Proof** (`#proof`): WhatsApp-style client feedback and verified public view counts directly linked to live posts.
8. **Your Tile** (`#your-tile`): An inviting empty grid tile acting as the primary conversion call to action.

## Visual & Aesthetic Direction
- **Palette**:
  - Warm Off-White: `#F4F1EA` (canvas)
  - Ink: `#0E0E0E` (text and strong graphic borders)
  - Accent: `#FFC400` (strictly used for the "Managed by Famebros" credit pill and highlighter marks, always paired with high-contrast black text)
- **Typography**:
  - Headlines: *Archivo* (variable width)
  - Body & Data: *Inter*
- **Design Language**:
  - Sharp corners (0 border radius)
  - Hairline borders (1px solid crisp lines)
  - Strictly no shadows, no color gradients, and no glassmorphism / blur effects

## Production Rules
- **Mobile-first**: Flawless experience on handheld screens and touch viewports.
- **Authenticity**: Never invent metrics, client names, or testimonials. No stock photography. Placeholders must look unambiguously like placeholders.
- **Playback**: Only one video plays at a time.
- **Accessibility & Motion**: Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. No hover-only interactions (all features operable on touch).
- **Data Integrity**: Dynamic client counters must always reflect `getLiveClients().length`, never hardcoded numbers.
